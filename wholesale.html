Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ) & Mix & Match

Our wholesale program allows you to mix and match different suncatcher models to meet the minimum total quantity requirement of 10 units. You are not required to purchase fixed packs of a single model.

Artisanal Production & Lead Times

Every piece is individually handcrafted in our studio in Argentina using real dried botanicals and resin. Orders are made to order. Standard production lead time is 7 to 15 business days depending on order volume.

Customs & Import Duties

International shipments are sent DAP (Delivered at Place). Import duties, taxes, or local clearance fees required by the destination country are the responsibility of the buyer.

How to Place a Wholesale Order

  • 1. Select Items: Browse our catalog and select quantities for your shop (minimum 10 total units).
  • 2. Complete Store Form: Fill out your business details and shipping destination.
  • 3. Submit Request: Send your order request directly to us.
  • 4. Order Confirmation: We verify inventory, calculate precise international shipping costs, and email/message you an official proforma invoice.
  • 5. Payment & Dispatch: Once payment is confirmed, we handcraft and dispatch your order with tracking details.

International Shipping

We ship worldwide directly from Argentina via reliable courier partners (DHL / FedEx / Express Postal Service). Shipping costs are calculated based on destination country, parcel weight, and dimensions. An exact shipping quote will be included in your order confirmation invoice.

Payment Methods

  • PayPal / Credit Card payment links (international fee may apply)

About Corre la Coneja

Corre la Coneja is an independent artisanal studio based in Argentina. We specialize in handcrafted resin suncatchers featuring original botanical compositions and high-clarity prisms. Each piece is meticulously crafted with carefully encapsulated flowers and natural elements, created to fill retail spaces with light, warmth, and singular artistic personality.
